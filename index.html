Discover University of Computer Studies, Pakokku

Welcome to UCS Pakokku

University of Computer Studies, Pakokku is dedicated to providing quality education in computer science and information technology.


Our university aims to develop skilled, creative and responsible students who can contribute to the development of technology and society.

Our Vision

To become a leading center of excellence in computer education, research and technological innovation.


Our Mission

To provide students with knowledge, practical skills and opportunities to become future IT professionals.
